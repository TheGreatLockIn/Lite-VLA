Epic 109

Benchmarking, Evaluation, and Observability

Measure latency, output validity, task success, safety behavior, and system reliability.

Intent | C4 context | Stories | ADR log

Epic Intent

Context: The paper emphasizes deployability and latency, so the project needs reproducible benchmarks and logs.

Scope: Benchmark scripts, metric definitions, simulation episodes, failure analysis, logging dashboards, and comparison tables.

Success criteria: Mean and p95 latency are reported; valid action rate is reported; simulation success rate is measured; failure cases are saved.
